● About the novel

The signal existed. The rest is where the story begins.

In Santa Maria, Brazil, Henrique uses the Wow! signal recorded in 1977 to test ARGOS, a system built to find patterns in imperfect data. He expects an error. Instead, he finds a pattern that survives every attempt to dismiss it.

But correlation is not proof — and separating coincidence from evidence begins to exact a human cost.
