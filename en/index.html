About the novel

The signal existed.
The rest is where the story begins.

In Santa Maria, Brazil, Henrique uses the Wow! signal recorded in 1977 to test ARGOS, a system built to find patterns in imperfect data. He expects an error. Instead, he finds a pattern that survives every attempt to dismiss it.

With Marina and Lara, the investigation crosses records, memories and events in Brazil. But correlation is not proof — and separating coincidence from evidence begins to exact a human cost.

The real starting point

The story began before the novel

On August 15, 1977, the Big Ear radio telescope in Ohio recorded a narrow, intense radio signal that lasted 72 seconds and was never detected again. Astronomer Jerry Ehman circled the sequence on the printout and wrote beside it: “Wow!”

  • Aug 15, 1977date of the record
  • Big EarOhio State University radio telescope
  • 72 sthe observation window
  • 6EQUJ5the circled sequence
