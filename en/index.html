About the novel

SIGNAL/NOISE

Novel · Available in English

In Santa Maria, Brazil, Henrique uses the Wow! signal recorded in 1977 to test ARGOS, a system built to find patterns in imperfect data. He expects an error. Instead, he finds a pattern that survives every attempt to dismiss it.

With Marina and Lara, the investigation crosses records, memories and events in Brazil. But correlation is not proof — and separating coincidence from evidence begins to exact a human cost.
