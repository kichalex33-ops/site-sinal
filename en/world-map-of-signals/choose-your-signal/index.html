How to take part

Four steps.

  1. 01

    Download

    Choose one of the official SIGNAL/NOISE posters.

  2. 02

    Print

    A4 is enough. Colour or black and white both work.

  3. 03

    Place the signal

    With permission from the venue, display it in a library, bookstore, café, university, school, cultural centre or another appropriate space.

  4. 04

    Register it

    Take a photo showing the poster at the location and send it to us with the venue name, city and country.

After confirmation, the location may appear on the World Map of Signals.
